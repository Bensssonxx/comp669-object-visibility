# Fresh test photos and controlled regional swaps

The November 10 results on previously studied photos motivate two follow-ups. This protocol precedes the first predictions in the new run. Use the two released systems, the same clean-trained probes, a 16-by-16 patch grid, gray fill, and 25% image visibility.

**Sampling.** Start from the official test split. Exclude every image ID and exact JPEG hash in the pilot, extension and annotated follow-up manifests, and duplicate JPEGs. Require the standard valid resized trimap. Sort IDs within breed by a fixed seeded hash. For a direct check, take the first 10 valid photos per breed and use the prior relocation algorithm, including its rejection rules and both seeds. Report exclusions. Separately take up to 10 photos per breed, in the same order, eligible for the regional experiment. Report counts for the whole candidate pool and the overlap. The two cohorts answer different questions. Eligibility uses annotations alone.

**Regional experiment.** A pure patch contains 196 pixels all marked foreground or all marked background. Keep mixed and uncertain patches fixed. Starting with the usual 64 visible center patches, hide four currently visible pure animal patches and reveal four hidden pure animal patches. Independently generate the equivalent four-patch exchange among pure background patches. Evaluate the center, animal swap alone, background swap alone, and both swaps together. Use the identical animal exchange in the two cells where it occurs and likewise for background. All four versions share image identity, fill, visible patch count and exact counts of visible foreground, background and uncertain pixels. Each single-region exchange changes eight patch positions. Reveal original pixel values without moving pixel content. A photo must have at least four eligible visible and four hidden patches in each region. The condition selects a subset of compositions. Describe its coverage before interpreting results.

**Analysis.** Average the two original relocation outcomes within each fresh photo before comparing against center. For each model the expected full-relocation directions are AIMv2 down and DINOv2 up. For the factorial analysis the primary question for each model is whether the net accuracy cost of changing the animal region exceeds the cost of changing the background. Compute animal cost by averaging the two one-factor drops across background settings, and background cost analogously. Their paired difference equals background-only accuracy minus animal-only accuracy. Report all cell means, both costs, and the difference. Report the two-factor interaction descriptively. For the two primary contrasts within each experiment use approximate simultaneous 95% paired, within-breed image-bootstrap intervals (10,000 draws, Bonferroni across two models). Also give pointwise intervals for the individual costs. Weight represented breeds equally. Keep comparisons conditional on these mask rules, selected photos and seeds. Reusing one fixed random swap per photo limits the inference over alternative swaps.

Masking in ViTs and foreground/background interventions have precedents in [Naseer et al.](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/c404a5adbf90e09631678b13b05d9d7a-Abstract.html) and [Xiao et al.](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=gl3D-xY7wLq). Here the regional test keeps the exact visible areas fixed and perturbs the same number of pure patches inside either region of the same pet picture.
